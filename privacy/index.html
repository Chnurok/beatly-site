Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Beatly is designed to work without an account. Practice data stays on your device.

Information Beatly stores

Beatly stores your display name, language, practice history, scores, streak, settings, and subscription entitlement locally on your device. The current release does not operate a Beatly cloud account or analytics backend.

Information Beatly does not collect

Beatly does not collect microphone recordings, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking data. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases

Beatly Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple In-App Purchase on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android. Apple or Google may process purchase and billing information under their own terms and privacy policies. Beatly stores only the local entitlement status required to unlock Pro features.

Data sharing and retention

Beatly does not sell personal information. Locally stored practice data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for Beatly.

Children

Beatly is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material policy updates will be posted on this page. Developer contact details will also be available in the official store listing before public release.
