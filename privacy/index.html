Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026

Beatly is designed to work without an account. Practice data stays on your device.

Information Beatly stores

Beatly stores your display name, language, practice history, scores, streak, and settings locally on your device. The current release does not operate a Beatly cloud account or analytics backend.

Information Beatly does not collect

Beatly does not collect microphone recordings, precise location, contacts, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking data. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Free release

Beatly 1.0 contains no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid-access state. Every exercise and feature is available without payment.

Data sharing and retention

Beatly does not sell personal information. Locally stored practice data remains until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for Beatly.

Children

Beatly is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material policy updates will be posted on this page. For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on Beatly's official Google Play or App Store listing.
