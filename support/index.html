Beatly Support

Quick answers for practice, sound, and progress.

No click sound?

  • Make sure media volume is on.
  • Disconnect Bluetooth audio devices you are not using.
  • Open Beatly Settings and confirm the beat volume and sound preset.
  • Restart the current session after an interruption.

Practice interrupted?

Beatly safely ends an active rhythm run when the app moves to the background, a call interrupts it, or audio focus changes. Return Home and start the exercise again.

Progress or language

Practice progress and preferences are stored locally. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Change English, Russian, or Spanish from Settings at any time.

Is everything free?

Yes. Version 1.0 has no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or daily limit. Every exercise, Song Mode, advanced drill, and training insight is available after onboarding.

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on Beatly's official Google Play or App Store listing.
